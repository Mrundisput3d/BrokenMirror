As Alliance Leader, the following policies are established to maintain fairness, discipline, and effective organization. These rules are strict and apply to all CP Leaders, and members. Respecting them ensures equal opportunity for every CP and keeps the alliance competitive and active.
ATTENDANCE POINTS
• Mid Epics (Orfen, Queen Ant, Zaken) = 1 pt per member • Baium = 2 pts per member • Valakas / Antharas = 3 pts Per member Caller : 9 pts for high epic participation / 6 pts for Baium participation / 3 pts for low epics participation . (Caller points are applied only for himself, and not for his CP)
1. Cooldown Protocol
- Low Epics: 1-week restriction before participating again. - Mid-Level Epics: 3-week restriction before participating again. - High Epics: 3-week restriction before participating again.
CP Member Limit
Each CP is allowed to have a maximum of 10 members in clan This rule exists so that other CPs also have the opportunity to maintain their 10 active members. Do not occupy extra spaces without permission. If a CP exceeds this limit without prior notice, penalty points will be applied.
Weekend Attendance
Major events take place during the weekend. If your CP has absent members for Saturday or Sunday, you must inform the Alliance Leader
Sunday Activity Requirement
Sunday is the most important active day for the clan. If a member cannot attend on Sunday, there is no problem, but the following must happen: - The member must notify their CP Leader in advance. - The member must temporarily leave the clan on Sunday. This allows the alliance to invite temporary drivers to fill the available spots. Members who leave for this reason will be invited back the next day.
Clan Picture & Activity Monitoring on Sundays
Clan activity is regularly monitored through clan pictures taken by the Alliance Leader. If during these checks: A CP is not 9/9 active (for example 6/9 or 7/9), and their members are still in the clan but offline on Sunday, then the following penalties will apply: - The CP will receive penalty points. - If this situation happens more than once, the entire CP score will be reset to 0 points. This ensures the alliance can replace inactive spots and remain competitive during key events.
Final Note
These policies are designed to maintain discipline, fairness, and strong performance across the alliance. Respecting these rules ensures every CP has equal opportunity and the alliance remains organized and competitive.
# External Clan Agreements
ExitGaming Clan Night Epics: - All night epics between 00:00 and 08:00 Greek time are assigned to ExitGaming. High Epics (Baium, Antharas, Valakas): - Distribution: 1 for ExitGaming, 3 for our alliance. - The order is determined by rolling a dice each time: - Example: 4 people roll → if ExitGaming wins, they take the first, next 3 are ours. - After each “raw” finishes, a new roll determines the next sequence. - Rolling continues until all parts of the epic are distributed fairly, alternating according to dice results.
Last Kingdom (LK) Clan Epic Distribution: - 1 mid epic and 2 low epics if they bring: - At least 5 parties of 9/9 for the weekend - And at least 3 parties of 9/9 on weekdays. - If they bring fewer parties, the deal is adjusted accordingly (e.g., 1 mid + 1 low, or only 1 low). Baium Access: - LK Clan has access to every 5th or 6th Baium.

ATTENDANCE POINTS
As Alliance Leader, the following policies are established to maintain fairness, discipline, and effective organization. These rules are strict and apply to all CP Leaders, and members. Respecting them ensures equal opportunity for every CP and keeps the alliance competitive and active. • Mid Epics (Orfen, Queen Ant, Zaken) = 1 pt per member • Baium = 2 pts per member • Valakas / Antharas = 3 pts Per member Caller : 9 pts for high epic participation / 6 pts for Baium participation / 3 pts for low epics participation . (Caller points are applied only for himself, and not for his CP)
CLAN LIMITS & COOLDOWNS
1. Cooldown Protocol - Low Epics: 1-week restriction before participating again. - Mid-Level Epics: 3-week restriction before participating again. - High Epics: 3-week restriction before participating again. CP Member Limit Each CP is allowed to have a maximum of 10 members in clan. This rule exists so that other CPs also have the opportunity to maintain their 10 active members. Do not occupy extra spaces without permission. If a CP exceeds this limit without prior notice, penalty points will be applied.
SUNDAY & WEEKEND RULES
Major events take place during the weekend. If your CP has absent members for Saturday or Sunday, you must inform the Alliance Leader. Sunday Activity Requirement Sunday is the most important active day for the clan. If a member cannot attend: - The member must notify their CP Leader in advance. - The member must temporarily leave the clan on Sunday. This allows the alliance to invite temporary drivers to fill the available spots. Members who leave for this reason will be invited back the next day. Clan Picture & Activity Monitoring: If during checks a CP is not 9/9 active and their members are offline: - The CP will receive penalty points. - If this happens more than once, the entire CP score will be reset to 0 points.

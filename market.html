As Alliance Leader, the following policies are established to maintain fairness, discipline, and effective organization.
01. ATTENDANCE POINTS
• Mid Epics (Orfen, Queen Ant, Zaken) = 1 pt per member • Baium = 2 pts per member • Valakas / Antharas = 3 pts Per member Caller : 9 pts for high epic participation / 6 pts for Baium participation / 3 pts for low epics participation. (Caller points are applied only for himself, and not for his CP) Ally leader : For every high epic earned, he cannot earn again for at least 6 months. For mid-low epics, and full drops, he can earn at any time, as long as the alliance approves during the weekly meeting.
02. Cooldown Protocol
- Low Epics: 1-week restriction before participating again. - Mid-Level Epics: 3-week restriction before participating again. - High Epics: 3-week restriction before participating again.
03. CP Member Limit
Each CP is allowed to have a maximum of 10 members in clan. Do not occupy extra spaces without permission.
04. Sunday Activity
Sunday is the most important active day. αν ένας member δεν μπορεί: - Notify CP Leader. - Temporarily leave the clan on Sunday for drivers. Penalties: If a CP is not 9/9 active on Sunday checks, penalty points apply. Repeat offense leads to score reset to 0.

ATTENDANCE POINTS
As Alliance Leader, the following policies are established to maintain fairness, discipline, and effective organization. These rules are strict and apply to all CP Leaders, and members. Respecting them ensures equal opportunity for every CP and keeps the alliance competitive and active. • Mid Epics (Orfen, Queen Ant, Zaken) = 1 pt per member • Baium = 2 pts per member • Valakas / Antharas = 3 pts Per member Caller : 9 pts for high epic participation / 6 pts for Baium participation / 3 pts for low epics participation . (Caller points are applied only for himself, and not for his CP)
EXTERNAL CLAN AGREEMENTS
# ExitGaming Clan Night Epics: - All night epics between 00:00 and 08:00 Greek time are assigned to ExitGaming. High Epics (Baium, Antharas, Valakas): - Distribution: 1 for ExitGaming, 3 for our alliance. - The order is determined by rolling a dice each time.
# Last Kingdom (LK) Clan Epic Distribution: - 1 mid epic and 2 low epics if they bring: At least 5 parties of 9/9 for the weekend and at least 3 parties of 9/9 on weekdays. - Baium Access: LK Clan has access to every 5th or 6th Baium.
SUNDAY & PENALTIES
Sunday is the most important active day. If a member cannot attend, they must notify their CP Leader and temporarily leave the clan on Sunday. Activity Monitoring: If a CP is not 9/9 active and members are offline on Sunday: - The CP will receive penalty points. - If this happens more than once, the entire CP score will be reset to 0 points.

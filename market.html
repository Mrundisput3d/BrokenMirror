ALLIANCE POLICIES
As Alliance Leader, the following policies are established to maintain fairness, discipline, and effective organization. These rules are strict and apply to all CP Leaders, and members. Respecting them ensures equal opportunity for every CP and keeps the alliance competitive and active.
ATTENDANCE POINTS
• Mid Epics (Orfen, Queen Ant, Zaken) = 1 pt per member
• Baium = 2 pts per member
• Valakas / Antharas = 3 pts Per member
Caller : 9 pts for high epic participation / 6 pts for Baium participation / 3 pts for low epics participation . (Caller points are applied only for himself, and not for his CP)
1. Cooldown Protocol
- Low Epics: 1-week restriction before participating again. - Mid-Level Epics: 3-week restriction before participating again. - High Epics: 3-week restriction before participating again.
CP Member Limit
Each CP is allowed to have a maximum of 10 members in clan. This rule exists so that other CPs also have the opportunity to maintain their 10 active members. Do not occupy extra spaces without permission. If a CP exceeds this limit without prior notice, penalty points will be applied.
Sunday Activity Requirement
Sunday is the most important active day for the clan. If a member cannot attend on Sunday, there is no problem, but the following must happen: - The member must notify their CP Leader in advance. - The member must temporarily leave the clan on Sunday. This allows the alliance to invite temporary drivers to fill the available spots.
Penalties
If during checks a CP is not 9/9 active and members are offline on Sunday:
- The CP will receive penalty points.
- If this situation happens more than once, the entire CP score will be reset to 0 points.
